Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What this site collects

This website does not ask for your name, email or phone number and has no sign-up. It does not set advertising cookies.

What stays in your browser

Your theme choice (light or dark) and language choice are saved in your own browser so the site remembers them. Nothing is sent to us.

Hosting

The site is served by a hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and time of visit for security and reliability.

Fonts and external links

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Links to Nimah MD, the Nimah API Hub, WhatsApp and Telegram open other services with their own privacy rules.

Contacting us

If you message Nimah Dev on WhatsApp, the message is handled by WhatsApp and is only used to reply to you.

Nimah MD and the API Hub

Those are separate sites. Check the page you are on for how it handles your data.
